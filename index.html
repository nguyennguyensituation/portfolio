About Me

I'm a Designer, Artist, Author, UX Writer, Product Manager,
Software Engineer.

I've spent a decade working in (almost) every role with (almost) everyone who has had a hand in getting software out into the world. I've been in the guts of the actual code—designing, building, and testing sustainable and scalable tech. And I've been involved at the higher level— developing roadmaps, running Agile teams, and communicating with stakeholders and customers.

The common thread among all these roles is that I got to collaborate with a lot of cool, smart, and wonderful people to build beautiful things out of nothing and solve problems A Few of My Favorite Problems

1. Frustrated by a confusing and inefficient new hire experience, I built an educational app to replace the old training program. It was used by hundreds of employees across multiple departments.

2. Tasked with teaching a team of non-engineers Git, I developed and led Git trainings based entirely around Post Malone's face tattoos. For months after, you could find little cutouts of Post Malone's face scattered in every corner of the office.

3. I once had to perform emergency mascot surgery to ensure that a certain national beer brand mascot would be ready in time for the Super Bowl. .
